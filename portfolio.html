Mostly curious.

Building

I like when an idea stops being hypothetical.

Sometimes an idea stays in my notes. Sometimes I accidentally turn it into a website.

I code, design interfaces, make small pieces of software, and think about how products should feel to use. I am still learning a lot of it, which is part of the fun. The point is less “look how technical I am” and more “I wanted this to exist, so I tried making it.”

06 / Direction

No deadline. Just direction.

Somewhere ahead.

I do not know the exact order of it yet. I just know the direction.

I want to build companies I actually care about. Make things people genuinely use. Tell stories. Make films. Write something worth keeping. Learn languages. Make music at least once. See a lot more of the world, and spend enough time somewhere new that it stops feeling like a trip.

I want the things I create to give me freedom, but more importantly, I want that freedom to mean something: time, experiences, and being able to take care of the people I love.

And I would like to still be making strange things on the internet when I am much older.
