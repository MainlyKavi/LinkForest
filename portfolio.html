01 / About

Mostly curious.
Sometimes too curious.

I like seeing what happens when an idea stops being a note and becomes a real thing. Sometimes that is a video. Sometimes an interface, a bit of code, some writing, or a strange internet project that does not really need a category.

I am still learning most of it. That is kind of the point. I care about making good things, but I do not want every part of my life to become content or a brand. I want people, places, stories, and enough room to keep getting obsessed with new things.

06 / Direction

No deadline. Just direction.

Somewhere ahead.

I don't know the exact order of it yet. I just know the direction.

I want to build companies I genuinely care about, make things people actually use, tell stories, make films, write something worth keeping, learn languages, make music at least once, and see a lot more of the world.

I want enough freedom to spend real time somewhere new, have experiences worth remembering, take care of the people I love, and still be making strange things on the internet when I am much older.
